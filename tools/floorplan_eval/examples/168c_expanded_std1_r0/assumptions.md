# Reconstruction assumptions

- Only `inputs/plan.jpg` was used. Dimensions on the image are millimeters; all geometry and JSON lengths are meters.
- The horizontal dimension chains conflict: the top totals **19.800 m**, while the bottom totals **20.500 m**. The bottom chain (3.840 + 2.860 + 3.460 + 8.840 + 1.500 m) governs the horizontal calibration, because it provides the bedroom divisions. Both sides give **11.510 m** overall depth. Piecewise interpolation aligns the traced wall axes with these dimensions; it compensates for the raster drawing not being uniformly to scale. The calibration anchors are recorded in `trace.json` and the build script.
- Wall locations follow the dominant drawn centrelines. Locally widened structural piers, double outlines and minor drafting offsets are regularized to a single wall axis. In particular, the bedroom/kitchen partition follows its continuous eastern line. Every modeled wall is **0.200 m thick and 2.300 m high**, including shaft partitions. Overall dimension values describe centreline extents; wall faces extend another 0.100 m at exterior axes. At junctions, horizontal wall courses own the shared volume and vertical courses meet their faces, avoiding overlapping solids while retaining the recorded centrelines.
- Plan coordinates have **x increasing to the right and z increasing toward the bottom of the image**. The origin is the intersection of the west exterior wall axis and the northernmost exterior wall axis, outside the stepped footprint. In the GLB, **Y is vertical** and floor elevation is **Y = 0**. Blender uses `(x, -plan_z, elevation)`; exporting with `export_yup=True` yields the same `(x, z)` plan frame as `elements.json`.
- Door and window widths in `elements.json` are the wall opening widths, including the frames. Swing openings are **0.900 m wide × 2.120 m high**, apart from the entrance at **1.000 × 2.300 m**. Frame clearances make the actual swing leaves slightly smaller. All door sills are zero. Swing leaves are shown open at 90 degrees to match the arcs; their object origins are at the hinge at floor level. Handles are children of the corresponding leaf.
- `hinge_side` is `start` or `end`, referencing the host wall's directed start-to-end axis. `swing_direction` is clockwise/counterclockwise in the plan view (x right, z down), from the closed leaf to its open position. Both fields are `null` for windows and sliders.
- Four short double-line openings adjacent to gray storage symbols are interpreted as **interior sliding doors**: west corridor storage, foyer storage, dressing storage, and the storage niche below the master bathroom. Their widths follow the drawing; height is **1.980 m**. The gray cabinet symbols themselves are omitted. These unlabeled symbols could instead represent service access glazing; the interpretation is recorded rather than inferred as certain.
- The master bedroom/east balcony connection and living room/south balcony connection are modeled as **two-panel glazed sliding doors, full height 2.300 m**, sill zero. All sliders are shown closed on two separate bypass tracks. Each panel is separately named `door_<n>_panel_<k>`.
- The eight other glazing symbols are modeled as windows, **1.750 m high with 0.425 m sills**, including the outer east balcony glazing and utility-room windows. Their widths are measured between the drawn ends of the openings. Window sash subdivision is represented by a central mullion because finer operability details cannot be resolved from the raster.
- The gray room above the east balcony is treated as a small service room, with its drawn swing door opening into the balcony. The enclosed area south of the kitchen is treated as a utility room, separate from the south balcony, with the shaft indicated by the crossed square enclosed by walls.
- Crossed boxes are interpreted as enclosed service shafts/voids; no access openings are added where none are drawn. No ceiling or roof is added, so the apartment interior remains visible.
- The thin double-line outer south balcony edge is interpreted as an **open guardrail**, rather than an opaque wall or an additional unmarked window. Its assumed height is **1.050 m**, with slender metal posts and glass infill. These two railing objects are distinct from the 2.300 m walls and are not included in the wall/opening JSON.
- The floor slab is 0.180 m deep including finishes, with finished room surfaces exactly at elevation zero. Floor colors distinguish rooms, circulation, bathrooms and balconies; material colors, rail finish, door finish and frame profiles are illustrative. Furniture, sanitary fixtures, cabinetry, outdoor equipment, building surroundings and ceilings are outside the requested wall/door/window reconstruction.

# Files and reproducibility

- `model.blend`: editable roofless apartment, materials, and two presentation cameras.
- `model.glb`: geometry and materials exported with `export_yup=True` and `export_apply=True`; cameras and lights excluded.
- `elements.json`: requested wall and opening records. Each `host_wall` references an existing wall object/record.
- `build_model.py`: complete reproducible bpy construction and export script.
- `trace.json`: dimensional calibration, wall/opening descriptions and gross floor-region areas. Region areas follow wall axes and are not net usable areas.
- `validation.json`: checks performed against the model and exported GLB.
- `verify_model.py`: independent saved-file checks, including 207 rays through the 23 openings and direct GLB vertex/transform checks for wall dimensions, floor elevation and hinge coordinates.
- `preview.png` and `top_view.png`: overview and plan renders of the finished model.
